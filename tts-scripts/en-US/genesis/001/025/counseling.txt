Genesis 1:25, counseling application. The text is 'God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that creeps on the ground according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

This passage can be applied to a sense of discrimination. The biblical principle is to hold onto the goodness according to kinds. A practical application is to suggest gratitude for diversity.
This passage can be applied to animal abuse. The biblical principle is to hold onto the responsibility of stewardship. A practical application is to suggest volunteering for pets.
This passage can be applied to the expansion of fear. The biblical principle is to hold onto the fact that even great beasts are creatures. A practical application is to suggest turning fear into prayer.
