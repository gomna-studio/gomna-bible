Genesis 1:25, historical background. The text is 'God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that creeps on the ground according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

We can consider the livestock economy. Cattle and sheep were the foundation of wealth and sacrifice. This point leads to the theology of resource dedication.
We can consider the reverence for wild animals. Lions and bears were symbols of royalty. This point leads to the idea that fear is also under sovereignty.
We can consider the worship of animal idols. The background of Egyptian bull worship is present. This point leads to the caution against creature worship.
