Genesis 1:25, theological meaning. The text is 'God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that creeps on the ground according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

The meaning of the goodness of diversity is revealed. The repetition of 'according to their kinds' shows the blessing of differences. Related to this, Psalm 104:24 can be considered.
The meaning of the stewardship mission is revealed. Regarding the responsibility of animal care, Proverbs 12:10 can be considered.
The meaning of creation affirmation is revealed. 'It was good' shows ecological value. Related to this, 1 Timothy 4:4 can be considered.
