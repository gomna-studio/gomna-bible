Genesis 1:25, original language analysis. The text is 'God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that creeps on the ground according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

The key expression in the text is 'made,' which shows God's meticulous touch.
The expression 'beasts of the earth' shows the diversity of the wild.
The expression 'livestock' shows the created order designed for cooperation with humans.
The expression 'creeps' shows the dignity of even small life.
The expression 'it was good' shows that God declared it good.
