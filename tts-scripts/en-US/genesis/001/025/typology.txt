Genesis 1:25, typology. The text is 'God made the beasts of the earth according to their kinds, the livestock according to their kinds, and everything that creeps on the ground according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

From the perspective of the sacrificial lamb, sheep among livestock are connected to the typology of atonement. This is linked to John 1:29.
From the perspective of the lion's judgment, it is connected to Christ's authority to judge. This is linked to Revelation 5:5.
From the perspective of new creation praise, it is connected to the chorus of all creatures. This is linked to Revelation 5:13.
