Genesis 1:23, typology. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.'

From the perspective of Pentecost, it connects with the grace of Pentecost (5). This is linked with Acts 2:1.
From the perspective of the five loaves, it connects with the provision of grace. This is linked with Mark 6:38.
From the perspective of the five talents, it connects with the stewardship of grace. This is linked with Matthew 25:15.
