Genesis 1:23, counseling application. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.'

This passage can be applied to daily monotony. The biblical principle is to hold onto daily newness. A practical application is to suggest three things to be thankful for.
This passage can be applied to time wastage. The biblical principle is to hold onto the gift of time. A practical application is to suggest a 15-minute plan.
This passage can be applied to an uncertain future. The biblical principle is to hold onto faithful repetition. A practical application is to suggest forming a routine.
