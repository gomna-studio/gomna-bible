Genesis 1:23, sermon material. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.'

The sermon title is The Fifth Day – Cycle of Grace. An example of application is applying The Fifth Day – Cycle of Grace in daily life.
In the introduction, it talks about a weekly planner. An example of application is a calendar.

Evening gratitude. It shows the blessing of closure (a confession of gratitude as we look back on the day). An example of application is a gratitude journal.
Preparation for rest. It shows the journey towards rest (a pilgrimage towards complete rest). An example of application is Saturday preparation.
In conclusion, it conveys the message to enjoy grace day by day. An example of application is manna.
