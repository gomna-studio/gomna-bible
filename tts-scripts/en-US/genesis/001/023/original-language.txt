Genesis 1:23, original language analysis. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.'

The key expression in the text is 'And there was evening,' showing the threshold of rest.
The expression 'And there was morning' shows the beginning of a new mission.
The expression 'day' shows that a day is a gift from God.
The expression 'fifth' shows that creation is moving orderly towards its climax.
The expression 'was' shows unchanging fulfillment.
