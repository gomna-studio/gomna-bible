Genesis 1:23, theological meaning. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fifth day.'

The meaning of grace time is revealed. The fifth shows the connection of grace. In relation, we can look at Ephesians 2:8.
The meaning of periodicity is revealed. Repetition shows faithfulness. In relation, we can look at Lamentations 3:23.
The meaning of expectation is revealed. It shows preparation for the sixth and rest. In relation, we can look at Hebrews 4:9.
