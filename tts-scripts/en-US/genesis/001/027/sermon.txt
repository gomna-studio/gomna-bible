Genesis 1:27, sermon material. The text is 'So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.'

The sermon title is Male and Female—The Dual Portrait of the Image. An application example is applying the dual portrait of the image in daily life.
The introduction talks about two pieces of a puzzle. An application example is a puzzle.
Equal value. It shows the equality of the image (both male and female have equal dignity as God's image). An application example is the balance of scales.
Different roles. It shows the gift of diversity (difference is not inferiority but beautiful complementarity). An application example is orchestra parts.
Purpose of unity. It shows the calling of partnership (a holy partnership to fulfill God's will together). An application example is the DNA double helix.
The conclusion delivers the message of a community restoring the image. An application example is joint service.
