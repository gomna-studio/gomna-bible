Genesis 1:27, typology. The text is 'So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.'

From the perspective of Christ-Church, it connects to the husband-wife model. This is linked to Ephesians 5:31-32.
From the perspective of new humanity, it connects to making the two one. This is linked to Ephesians 2:15.
From the perspective of the marriage of the Lamb, it connects to ultimate union. This is linked to Revelation 19:7.
