Genesis 1:27, original language analysis. The text is 'So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.'

The key verb in the text is “created (bara).” It shows creation ex nihilo.
The expression “in his image” shows God-centered identity.
The expression “mankind” shows the representative of humanity.
The expression “male” shows the goodness of gender.
The expression “female” shows complementary equality.
