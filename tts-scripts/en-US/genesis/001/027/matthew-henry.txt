Genesis 1:27, Matthew Henry. The text is 'So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.'

Matthew Henry's English original text.
Woman was made from man's side.
The key here is that it shows intimacy and equality.
Second English original text.
Image of God lies in the soul.
The key here is that it shows the emphasis on spirituality.
Final English original text.
In Christ the image is renewed.
The key here is that it shows redemption and restoration.
