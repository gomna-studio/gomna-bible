Genesis 1:27, theological meaning. The text is 'So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.'

The meaning of gender equality is revealed. Both male and female are in the image—showing equal dignity. Related to this, Galatians 3:28 can be considered.
The meaning of relationality is revealed. Multiple genders imply sociality. Related to this, Genesis 2:18 can be considered.
The meaning of creative gender is revealed. Gender shows the order of creation. Related to this, Matthew 19:4 can be considered.
