Genesis 1:17, theological meaning. The text is 'God set them in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth.'

The meaning of providential arrangement is revealed. The setting of creation's position and orbit shows providence. Related to this, see Psalm 8:3.
The meaning of mission's universality is revealed. The illumination of the whole earth shows the universality of the gospel. Related to this, see Acts 1:8.
The meaning of dependent existence is revealed. The reason for the luminaries' existence is illumination, showing teleology. Related to this, see Ephesians 2:10.
