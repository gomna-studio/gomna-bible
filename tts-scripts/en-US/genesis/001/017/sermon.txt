Genesis 1:17, sermon material. The text is 'God set them in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth.'

The sermon title is Position and Mission—Arrangement of the Expanse. An example of application is applying Position and Mission—Arrangement of the Expanse in daily life.
The introduction discusses the celestial map. An example of application is the planetarium experience.
The sovereignty of arrangement. It shows God's design (all existence is providentially arranged, not by chance). An example of application is moving pieces on a chessboard.
The purpose of illumination. It shows the mission for others (light exists for the earth, not itself). An example of application is streetlights for city safety.
If it doesn't flow, it perishes. It shows the blessing of sharing (grace received must flow to maintain vitality). An example of application is the Dead Sea vs. Galilee.
The conclusion delivers the message to shine in your place. An example of application is the 'Luminous Body' campaign at work.
