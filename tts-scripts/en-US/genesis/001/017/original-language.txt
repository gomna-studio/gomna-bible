Genesis 1:17, original language analysis. The text is 'God set them in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth.'

Vayiten is a perfect verb meaning 'to set.' The arrangement shows God's design.
Birkiya is a noun meaning 'in the expanse.' It shows the framework of order.
Leha'ir means 'to give light,' and is a Hiphil infinitive. Light must flow to show meaning.
Al-ha'aretz means 'on the earth,' and is a prepositional phrase. The mission target shows the world.
Otham is a noun meaning 'them.' It shows the lack of creaturely autonomy.
