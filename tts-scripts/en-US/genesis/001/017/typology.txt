Genesis 1:17, typology. The text is 'God set them in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth.'

From the perspective of the church as a lampstand, it connects to Rev 1:20 with the seven stars and lampstands. This relates to the local church illuminating the world.
From the perspective of saints as the light of the city, it connects to Matt 5:14-16 with the lamp on a stand. This relates to glorifying through good deeds.
From the perspective of the completion of the new creation, the sun and moon are unnecessary—direct illumination connects to Rev 21:23.
