Genesis 1:9, typology. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear: and it was so.'

From the perspective of OT type, crossing dry land through the parted Red Sea. In connection with Christ, Baptism and salvation.
From the perspective of NT fulfillment, promise of a new earth. In connection with Christ, Rev 21:1.
From the perspective of Christology, a house built on the rock. In connection with Christ, Matt 7:24-25.
