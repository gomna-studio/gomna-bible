Genesis 1:9, historical background. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear: and it was so.'

We can consider Ancient fear of the sea. The sea as a chaos power. Overcome fear.
We can consider Settled agriculture. The need for dry land. Blessing of stability.
We can consider Contrast with Noah. Waters gathered after the flood. A pattern of re-creation.
