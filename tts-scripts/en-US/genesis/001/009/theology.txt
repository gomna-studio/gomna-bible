Genesis 1:9, theological meaning. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear: and it was so.'

Order of creation is revealed here. Boundaries are set. This can be seen along with Job 38:8-11.
Priority of grace is revealed here. Dry land appears as the stage for life. This can be seen along with Isa 45:12.
Redemption and re-creation is revealed here. Parallel with Noah and the Exodus. This can be seen along with 1 Pet 3:20-21.
