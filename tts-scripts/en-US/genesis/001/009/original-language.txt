Genesis 1:9, key word analysis. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear: and it was so.'

Yiqqavu is a niphal imperative meaning be gathered. Providence that gathers.
Mayim is a noun meaning waters. From chaos to order.
Nir’eh is a niphal jussive meaning appear. Hidden potential revealed.
Yabbashah is a noun meaning dry land. A stable foundation.
Maqom ehad is a phrase meaning one place. The principle of concentration.

This verse shows that God gathers the waters and reveals dry land by His commanding word.
