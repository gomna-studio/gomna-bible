Genesis 1:1, historical background. The text is 'In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.'

In the ancient Near Eastern world, there were various stories explaining the beginning of the world. Myths like the Babylonian Enuma Elish say the world came into being through the conflicts and battles of gods. But the Bible shows a completely different path. The world was not born from the strife of gods, but was created by the one true God with purpose.

In Jewish tradition, the phrase 'In the beginning' was sometimes understood in connection with wisdom, and also interpreted in relation to the Word. This is deeply connected to the confession in the Gospel of John, 'In the beginning was the Word.'

Also, ancient texts contain similar memories about floods or creation. However, even among those stories, the Bible speaks clearly. The world is not the result of chance or chaos, but an orderly world created by one God.

Thus, Genesis 1:1 is a uniquely shining confession of faith even within the ancient world. This verse tells us that our faith is not based on vague imagination, but on a clear trust in the Creator God.
