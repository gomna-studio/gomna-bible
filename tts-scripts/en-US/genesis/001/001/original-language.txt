Genesis chapter 1, verse 1 — this is the original-language analysis. The text is, ‘In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.’

This verse begins with the word “bereshit.” It means “In the beginning.” Every created thing has a beginning, but this shows that God existed before the beginning itself.

“Elohim” is a word that refers to God. It takes a plural form, yet it points to the one God, and it leads us to reflect on the deep and mysterious being of God to which the whole Bible bears witness.

“bara” means to create. People make things from materials that already exist, but God is the Creator who brings into being what was not.

“hashamayim” means the heavens. Not only the sky we see, but even the unseen spiritual world — all of it belongs within God’s creation.

“ha'aretz” means the earth. This earth on which we walk and live did not come about by chance; it is a created thing formed within God’s will and plan.

Therefore Genesis chapter 1, verse 1 explains the beginning of the world, and at the same time quietly declares that God is the source of all that exists.
