Genesis 1:1, typology. The text is 'In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.'

The first creation in the Bible points to the new creation to come. God establishes order in chaos, gives light in darkness, and fills emptiness with life. This resembles God's way of renewing people in sin and death through salvation.

The New Testament reveals this light of creation again in Jesus Christ. The Gospel of John begins with 'In the beginning was the Word,' linking the beginning in Genesis to Christ. The Word who created the world became flesh and dwelt among us.

Second Corinthians 5:17 says if anyone is in Christ, they are a new creation. If the first creation is the beginning of all existence, the new creation in Christ is the beginning of a saved life.

Colossians 1:16 also testifies that all things were created by and for Christ. Therefore, Genesis 1:1 does not only speak of past creation. It foreshadows God's redemptive work of renewal in Christ.
