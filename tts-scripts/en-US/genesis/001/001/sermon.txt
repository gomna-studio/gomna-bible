Genesis chapter 1, verse 1, sermon material. The text is 'In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.'

The sermon title for this message can be 'The God of the Beginning, the God of Today.' People ask many questions about the origin of the universe. Science tries to explain how the world operates, but the Bible answers deeper questions. Questions of who created this world and why.

First, God is the one who existed before the beginning. Before time, space, or any created thing, God already was. Just as a clockmaker exists outside of the clock, God, who made time, is not confined by it.

Second, God is the almighty creator. God created something out of nothing. Even in situations that seem impossible to us, God can open a new way.

Third, God creates with purpose. Every creature exists not by chance, but was made within God's will. Therefore, our lives are not thrown into existence without meaning.

The conclusion is this. The God who created the heavens and the earth in the beginning is still at work today. When we entrust our chaos and emptiness to Him, He establishes order and life within them.
