Genesis chapter 1 verse 1, cross-reference. The text is 'In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.'

The Bible in many places testifies that God is the Creator.

Psalm 33 verse 6 says that by the word of the Lord the heavens were made. Creation was not a laborious task for God, but an act accomplished by His word.

Isaiah 45 verse 12 also declares that God made the earth and created man upon it. This is a confession that the earth we live on and our existence come from God's hand.

Psalm 104 verse 24 praises that God has done many works and made them all in wisdom. The created world shows not only God's power but also His wisdom.

The New Testament connects this creation with Christ. John chapter 1 verses 1 to 3 testify that in the beginning was the Word, and all things were made through Him. Colossians 1 verse 16 also says that all things were created in Christ.

Hebrews 11 verse 3 says that by faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God. And Revelation 4 verse 11 praises that God is worthy to receive glory and honor because He created all things.

Thus, the entire Bible speaks with one voice. God is the Creator, and everything begins with Him and returns to Him.
