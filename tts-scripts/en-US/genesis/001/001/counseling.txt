Genesis chapter 1 verse 1, counseling application. The text is 'In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.'

If you're asking why you exist or what meaning your life holds, this passage quietly offers comfort. You are not a product of chance. You are a precious person created by God with intention and purpose.

For those who feel the need for a fresh start, this passage brings hope. God is the one who created something out of nothing. Even in places that seem like there's nothing left, God can help you begin anew. Your past failures are not your final story.

Even when it feels like you've lost control of your life and everything seems chaotic, you can remember the Creator God. God is the one who made the heavens and the earth, and He still governs the whole world. You can bring your inner chaos and life's anxieties before God.

Genesis chapter 1 verse 1 doesn't just speak of the beginning of the world. It tells you that your life also began in God and can be rebuilt in God.
