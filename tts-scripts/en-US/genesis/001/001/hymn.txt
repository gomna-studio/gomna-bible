Genesis 1:1, this is a hymn. The text is 'In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.'

Hymns that are good to meditate on with this verse are those that exalt the Creator God.

First, hymn 64, 'Praise to the Lord, the Almighty' is a hymn of joyful worship to God who made the whole world. It captures the gratitude and praise of creatures before the Creator.

Hymn 78, 'The Spacious Firmament on High' leads us to meditate on the Creator's handiwork as we gaze at the sky. It reminds us that the sky we see every day is part of God's creation.

Hymn 40, 'How Great Thou Art' praises God's greatness through nature and all creation. It is the hymn most directly connected to the confession of Genesis 1:1.

Also, hymn 472, 'Fairest Lord Jesus' exalts the name of the Creator God, reminding us that the source of all beauty is in God.

When meditating on Genesis 1:1, we move beyond explanation to praise. The world is God's masterpiece, and our lives are in His hands.
