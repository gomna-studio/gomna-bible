Genesis 1:11, Matthew Henry. The text is 'Then God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, and fruit trees on the earth bearing fruit after their kind with seed in them"; and it was so.'

Matthew Henry's English text.
Herbs and fruits were made for man before man was made.
Before creating man, herbs and fruits were prepared. The key point here is that it shows prevenient grace.
Second English text.
Every creature is serviceable in its place and kind.
Every creature is beneficial in its place. The key point here is that it shows the diversity of ministry.
Final English text.
We must bring forth fruit according to our kind.
We too must bear fruit according to our kind. The key point here is that it shows identity fruit.
