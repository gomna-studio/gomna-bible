Genesis 1:11, original language analysis. The text is 'Then God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, and fruit trees on the earth bearing fruit after their kind with seed in them"; and it was so.'

Tadshe is a perfect verb meaning "to sprout." The activation of life shows God's command.
Deshe is a noun meaning "grass." Even the smallest life shows God's work.
Eseb is a noun meaning "herb." Food shows God's provision.
Zera is a noun meaning "seed." It shows the present holding the future.
Pri is a noun meaning "fruit." The result shows the seed's obedience.
