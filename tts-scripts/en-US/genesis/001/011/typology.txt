Genesis 1:11, typology. The text is 'Then God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, and fruit trees on the earth bearing fruit after their kind with seed in them"; and it was so.'

From the perspective of the fruit of the Spirit, it connects with the seed-to-fruit process. This is linked to Galatians 5:22-23.
From the perspective of the word seed, it connects with the parable of the good soil in Matthew 13:23. This relates to the gospel of Christ.
From the perspective of the firstfruits of resurrection, it connects with the concept of firstfruits. This is linked to 1 Corinthians 15:20.
