Genesis 1:11, hymn. The text is 'Then God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, and fruit trees on the earth bearing fruit after their kind with seed in them"; and it was so.'

New Hymnal 305, 'Amazing Grace,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in that it evokes a confession of the fruit of grace.
New Hymnal 492, 'The Farmer Goes Out to the Field,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in that it evokes the image of sowing and harvesting.
New Hymnal 588, 'Consider the Birds of the Air,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in that it evokes trust in God's provision.
New Hymnal 496, 'Flowers,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in that it evokes praise and diversity of creation.
