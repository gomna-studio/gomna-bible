Genesis 1:11, historical background. The text is 'Then God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, and fruit trees on the earth bearing fruit after their kind with seed in them"; and it was so.'

We can consider the ancient agrarian society. The creation of plants explains the basis of livelihood and economy. This point makes us think of God as the ultimate provider.
We can consider the concept of 'after their kind.' It reflects the emphasis on genealogy and lineage in Near Eastern culture. This point emphasizes order within diversity.
We can consider herbal medicine. Plants as medicine, fragrance, and sacrificial materials provide a background. This point suggests a sermon on the healing resources of the created ecosystem.
