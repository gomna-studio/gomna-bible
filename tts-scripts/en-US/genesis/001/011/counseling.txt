Genesis 1:11, counseling application. The text is 'Then God said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, and fruit trees on the earth bearing fruit after their kind with seed in them"; and it was so.'

This passage can be applied to those discouraged by fruitlessness. The biblical principle is to hold onto the time difference between sowing and reaping. A practical application is to suggest writing a growth journal.
This passage can be applied to those with identity confusion. The biblical principle is to hold onto 'after their kind.' A practical application is to suggest taking a spiritual gifts test.
This passage can be applied to those with material worries. The biblical principle is to hold onto providential supply. A practical application is to suggest practicing gratitude at the dinner table.
