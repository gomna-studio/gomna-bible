Genesis 1:26, hymn. The text is 'Then God said, "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. And let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth."'

New Hymnal 94, 'Joy to the World,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text in that it reminds us of Restoration of Image.
New Hymnal 31, 'Praise Him! Praise Him!,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text in that it reminds us of Praise to the Creator.
New Hymnal 496, 'Flowers (original title: Hana mo),' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text in that it reminds us of Humans Among Creatures.
New Hymnal 182, 'I've Got Peace Like a River,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text in that it reminds us of Grace of Restoration.
