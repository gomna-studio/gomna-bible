Genesis 1:26, original language analysis. The text is 'Then God said, "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. And let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth."'

The key expression in the text is "Let us make," showing a Trinitarian council.
Another key expression is "image," showing essential reflection.
The expression "likeness" shows a representative image.
The expression "man" shows a being of dust and spirit.
The expression "let them have dominion" shows delegated rule.
