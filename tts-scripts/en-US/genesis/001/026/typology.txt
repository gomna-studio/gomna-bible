Genesis 1:26, typology. The text is 'Then God said, "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness. And let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the livestock and over all the earth and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth."'

From the perspective of Christ—the true image, it connects to the invisible image of God. This is linked to Colossians 1:15.
From the perspective of the new man, it connects to the restoration of the image. This is linked to Ephesians 4:24.
From the perspective of the kingdom rule, it connects to the saints reigning. This is linked to Revelation 22:5.
