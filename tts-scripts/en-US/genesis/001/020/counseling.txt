Genesis 1:20, counseling application. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that hath life, and fowl that may fly above the earth in the open firmament of heaven.'

This word can be applied to those without fruit. The biblical principle is to hold onto the command to multiply. A practical application is to suggest a small goal of '1+1 growth.'
This word can be applied to those with a closed life. The biblical principle is to hold onto spatial expansion within order. A practical application is to suggest trying new hobbies and relationships.
This word can be applied to those with dryness in praise. The biblical principle is to hold onto the song of the birds. A practical application is to suggest three minutes of gratitude praise daily.
