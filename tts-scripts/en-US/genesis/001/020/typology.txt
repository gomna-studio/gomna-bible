Genesis 1:20, typology. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that hath life, and fowl that may fly above the earth in the open firmament of heaven.'

From the perspective of the Holy Spirit dove, it connects to the dove at the dawn of new creation. This relates to Matthew 3:16.
From the perspective of the fish in the feeding of the 5,000, it connects to the abundant filling by sea creatures. This relates to John 6:11.
From the perspective of the gospel net, it connects to the catching of many fish. This relates to Luke 5:6-10.
