Genesis 1:20, original language analysis. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that hath life, and fowl that may fly above the earth in the open firmament of heaven.'

יִשְׁרְצ֣וּ is a perfect verb meaning 'to swarm.' The abundance of life shows God's joy.
הַמַּ֔יִם means 'the waters,' a definite plural. Even the chaotic sea shows the cradle of life.
שֶׁ֖רֶץ נֶ֣פֶשׁ חַיָּ֑ה is a noun meaning 'living creature that moves.' Even the small shows precious life.
יִעֹ֣ופ is a perfect verb meaning 'to fly.' Freedom shows the gift of creation.
עַ֥ל פְּנֵ֖י רְקִ֣יעַ means 'above the surface of the firmament,' a prepositional phrase. It shows the order of spatial distribution.
