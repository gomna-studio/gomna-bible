Genesis 1:20, theological meaning. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that hath life, and fowl that may fly above the earth in the open firmament of heaven.'

The meaning of the doctrine of life is revealed. Sea and air creatures also show God's breath. In relation, we can look at Psalm 104:25-30.
The meaning of the command to multiply is revealed. Be fruitful and multiply shows the blessing of creation. In relation, we can look at Genesis 9:1.
The meaning of spatial theology is revealed. It shows the mission of each domain of land, sea, and air. In relation, we can look at Ephesians 4:10.
