Genesis 1:20, historical background. The text is 'And God said, Let the waters bring forth abundantly the moving creature that hath life, and fowl that may fly above the earth in the open firmament of heaven.'

We can consider the contrast with the Tihon myth. The background is the peaceful birth of life, not a sea monster myth. This makes us think of creation by the Word, not violence or evolution.
We can consider the symbolism of birds. The background is the symbol of ancient kingship and messengers. This makes us think of the parable of spreading the gospel.
We can consider fishing and bird rearing. The background is the fishing in Galilee, Israel, and dove sacrifices. This makes us think of God as the provider of livelihood.
