Genesis 1:29, sermon material. The text is 'And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.'

The sermon title is Seeds and Fruits—God's Table. An example of application is applying Seeds and Fruits—God's Table in daily life.
The introduction talks about the organic market. An example of application is a farmer interview.
God is the giver. He shows the Creator as the provider (God as the source of all food). An example of application is children's lunchboxes.
The future of seeds. Seeds containing the future (God's plan extending to the next generation). An example of application is a seed bank.
The table of thanksgiving. A meal of grace (confessing God's goodness at every meal). An example of application is a prayer of thanks.
The conclusion conveys the message that food is also a mission. An example of application is a food bank.
