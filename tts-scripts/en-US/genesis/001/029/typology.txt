Genesis 1:29, typology. The text is 'And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.'

From the perspective of manna, it connects to the heavenly food. This is also linked to John 6:32.
From the perspective of the fruit of the tree of life, it connects to the announcement of the food of eternal life. This is also linked to Revelation 22:2.
From the perspective of the Eucharist, it connects to the new covenant of bread and wine. This is also linked to Luke 22:19.
