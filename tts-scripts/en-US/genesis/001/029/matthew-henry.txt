Genesis 1:29, Matthew Henry. The text is 'And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.'

Matthew Henry's English original text.
God first feeds before commands.
He feeds before commands. The key here is that it shows preceding grace.
Second English original text.
Common mercies are distinguished favors.
Everyday mercies are special favors. The key here is that it shows a habit of gratitude.
Last English original text.
Eat freely but bless reverently.
Eat freely but give thanks reverently. The key here is that it shows table piety.
