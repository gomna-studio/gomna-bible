Genesis 1:29, theological meaning. The text is 'And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.'

The meaning of providential supply is revealed. Food also shows God's grace. In this regard, we can look at Matthew 6:31.
The meaning of the steward's table is revealed. Food choice shows an act of faith. In this regard, we can look at 1 Corinthians 10:31.
The meaning of food in creation order is revealed. It shows peace and non-violence before the fall. In this regard, we can look at Isaiah 11:7.
