Genesis 1:29, original language analysis. The text is 'And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.'

The key verb in the text means 'I have given.' It shows God as the provider.
The expression 'seed' shows provision including future generations.
The expression 'herb' shows health and sustainability.
The expression 'fruit' shows taste and abundance.
The expression 'to you' shows a relational gift.
