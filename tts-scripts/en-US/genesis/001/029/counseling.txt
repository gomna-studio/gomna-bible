Genesis 1:29, counseling application. The text is 'And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.'

This verse can be applied to material worries. The biblical principle is to hold onto the promise of provision. A practical application is to suggest a prayer for a list of needs.
This verse can be applied to eating habit addictions. The biblical principle is to hold onto the steward's table. A practical application is to suggest the Daniel Fast.
This verse can be applied to environmental food issues. The biblical principle is to hold onto the sustainability of seeds and fruits. A practical application is to suggest local food.
