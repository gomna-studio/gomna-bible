Genesis 1:30, original language analysis. The text is 'And to every beast of the earth and to every bird of the heavens and to everything that has the breath of life, I have given every green plant for food.' And it was so.

Yereq is a noun meaning 'green plant.' It shows the universality of provision.
Okhlah is a noun meaning 'food.' It shows the fulfillment of basic needs.
Lekol is a noun meaning 'to everyone.' It shows grace without discrimination.
Hayyah means 'living thing.' It shows the community of life.
Vayehi-ken means 'and it was so.' It shows the realization of the word.
