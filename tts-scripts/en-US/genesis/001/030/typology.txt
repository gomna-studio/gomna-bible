Genesis 1:30, typology. The text is 'And to every beast of the earth and to every bird of the heavens and to everything that has the breath of life, I have given every green plant for food.' And it was so.

From the perspective of the meadow kingdom, it connects to the peace of the new heavens and new earth. This is also linked to Revelation 21:4.
From the perspective of the Good Shepherd, it connects to leading to green pastures. This is also linked to John 10:9.
From the perspective of the feeding of the five thousand on the grass, it connects to seating on green grass. This is also linked to Mark 6:39.
