Genesis 1:30, theological meaning. The text is 'And to every beast of the earth and to every bird of the heavens and to everything that has the breath of life, I have given every green plant for food.' And it was so.

The meaning of ecological peace is revealed. It shows a table without bloodshed. Relatedly, Isaiah 11:6-7 can be viewed together.
The meaning of equitable provision is revealed. It shows to all creatures. Relatedly, Psalm 104:27-28 can be viewed together.
The meaning of the fulfillment of the word is revealed. It shows the repetition of 'and it was so.' Relatedly, Isaiah 55:11 can be viewed together.
