Genesis 1:18, original language analysis. The text is 'to govern the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.'

Limshol means 'to govern,' and it is a Qal infinitive. It shows delegated rule.
Hayom is a noun meaning 'day.' It symbolizes activity and life.
Hallayla is a noun meaning 'night.' It symbolizes rest and mystery.
Ulehavdil means 'to divide,' and it is a Hiphil infinitive construct. It shows holiness=separation.
Ki-Tov is an adjective meaning 'it was good.' It shows good order by God's standard.
