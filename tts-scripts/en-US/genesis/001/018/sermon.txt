Genesis 1:18, sermon material. The text is 'to govern the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.'

The sermon title is Light and Darkness—Grace of Distinction. An example of application is applying the grace of distinction between light and darkness in daily life.
The introduction talks about the process of a photo darkroom and development. An example of application is negative film.
The rule of light. It shows the authority of the day (God governing the time of activity and productivity). An example of application is a solar alarm clock.
The role of darkness. It shows the grace of the night (the gift of darkness given for rest and recovery). An example of application is the sleep hormone melatonin.
Holy distinction. It shows the wisdom of discernment (living a life that clearly distinguishes good and evil like light and darkness). An example of application is the library classification system.
The conclusion delivers the message to choose goodness. An example of application is spam mail filtering.
