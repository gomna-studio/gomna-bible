Genesis 1:18, theological meaning. The text is 'to govern the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.'

The meaning of ethical distinction is revealed. The division of light and darkness shows the basis for the good and evil schema. Relatedly, Ephesians 5:8-11 can be viewed together.
The meaning of balanced spirituality is revealed. The governance of day and night shows the balance of work and rest. Relatedly, Mark 6:31 can be viewed together.
The meaning of creation's goodness is revealed. God's evaluation standard—objective goodness is shown. Relatedly, James 1:17 can be viewed together.
