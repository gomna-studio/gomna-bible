Genesis 1:18, typology. The text is 'to govern the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.'

From the perspective of the darkness of the cross, darkness during the day connects with judgment distinction. This connects with Luke 23:44.
From the perspective of the resurrection dawn, the end of darkness connects with new light. This connects with John 20:1.
From the perspective of the New Jerusalem, there is no night. This connects with Revelation 22:5.
