Genesis 1:18, Matthew Henry. The text is 'to govern the day and the night, and to divide the light from the darkness. And God saw that it was good.'

Matthew Henry's original English text.
Day and night are tutors to duty and devotion.
Day and night serve as teachers, guiding us in our responsibilities and spiritual practices. The key here is that it shows Time spirituality.
Second original English text.
Light and darkness preach to us the difference between good and evil.
Light and darkness illustrate the contrast between good and evil. The key here is that it shows Natural ethics.
Final original English text.
God saw it good; so must we acquiesce.
Since God deemed it good, we should also accept it willingly. The key here is that it shows Conformity and gratitude.
