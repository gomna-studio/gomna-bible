Genesis 1:16, typology. The text says, 'God made two great lights—the greater light to govern the day and the lesser light to govern the night—and also made the stars.'

From the perspective of Christ—the Evening Star—this connects to Rev 22:16, where Jesus comes as the Morning Star. This relates to the guidance of the Lord who is the Light.
From the perspective of the Church—the Moon—the moon reflects the sun’s light just as the Church reflects the light of Christ. This also connects to 2 Corinthians 3:18.
From the perspective of the saints—the Stars—this relates to Dan 12:3, shining like stars. This connects to the glory of gospel proclamation.
