Genesis 1:16, historical background. The text says, 'God made two great lights—the greater light to govern the day and the lesser light to govern the night—and also made the stars.'

Consider the deification of the sun and moon. There is a background of widespread celestial worship such as Egypt's Ra and Babylon's Shamash. This point suggests providing a gospel-centered creation argument within an idolatrous culture.
Consider the refutation of astrology. There is a background of rejecting the custom of divining fate by the positions of the stars. This point suggests interpreting the future through God's sovereignty.
Consider the agricultural calendar. There is a background of sowing and harvesting according to the cycles of sunlight and moonlight. This point suggests preaching on restoring the rhythm of work and rest.
