Genesis 1:16, Matthew Henry. The text is, 'God made two great lights—the greater light to govern the day and the lesser light to govern the night—and also made the stars.'

This is the original English text from Matthew Henry.
The lights are servants, not deities.
The lights in the sky are servants, not gods. The key point here is to show the prohibition of creature worship.
Second original English text.
