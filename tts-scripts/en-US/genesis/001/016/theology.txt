Genesis 1:16, theological meaning. The text says, 'God made two great lights—the greater light to govern the day and the lesser light to govern the night—and also made the stars.'

The meaning of delegated order in creation is revealed. Delegation of day and night governance to the lights—God shows Himself as sovereign. Related passages include Psalm 136:7-9.
The meaning of hierarchical roles is revealed. The greater and lesser lights show mutual complementarity, not superiority or inferiority. Related passages include Romans 12:4-5.
The meaning of cosmic revelation is revealed. The stars testify as greater and lesser witnesses. Related passages include Psalm 19:1.
