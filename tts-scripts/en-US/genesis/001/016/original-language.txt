Genesis 1:16, original language analysis. The text reads, 'And God made two great lights: the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night; He also made the stars.'

Me'orot is a noun meaning 'lights.' Light is not a source but a reflector—the Creator shows the source.
Ha'gadol is an adjective meaning 'great (light).' The greatness of the sun also shows the limits of creation.
Ha'katon is an adjective meaning 'small (light).' Even small roles show essential pieces of the puzzle.
Le'memshelet is a noun meaning 'dominion.' Authority shows what is delegated.
Kochavim is a noun meaning 'stars.' It shows grace that cannot be counted.
