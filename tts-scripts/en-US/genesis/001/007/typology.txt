Genesis 1:7, typology. The text is 'And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.'

From the perspective of OT type, closing the windows in Noah’s flood. In connection with Christ, Final judgment.
From the perspective of NT fulfillment, heaven opened at the Spirit’s coming. In connection with Christ, Acts 2:2.
From the perspective of Christology, christ who gives living water. In connection with Christ, John 7:37-39.
