Genesis 1:7, historical background. The text is 'And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.'

We can consider Understanding of climate. The idea of heavenly water-channels. Rain as blessing.
We can consider Agrarian society. Dependence on rainy and dry seasons. Pray for the opening of the heavens.
We can consider Background mythology. Baal as a rain-god. Contrast with Yahweh, the true Provider.
