Genesis 1:7, theological meaning. The text is 'And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.'

God the Provider is revealed here. The waters above as the source of rain. This can be seen along with Deut 11:13-15.
Distinction and order is revealed here. The act of separation is repeated. This can be seen along with Isa 45:12.
Preservation of creation is revealed here. Ongoing preservation (providence). This can be seen along with Col 1:17.
