Genesis 1:7, sermon material. The text is 'And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.'

The sermon title is 'Waters above and below: the mystery of provision.' An example of application is a sprinkler system.
The introduction addresses this: A farmer’s prayer. An example of application is drought news.
The first theme is this. Grace prepared in advance. An example of application is a reservoir.
The second theme is this. The God who upholds—not only creates but preserves by providence. An example of application is managing a city dam.
The third theme is this. The water of life—Christ gives water so that we never thirst again. An example of application is a water filter.
The conclusion is this. A life that is no longer thirsty. An example of application is an oasis.
