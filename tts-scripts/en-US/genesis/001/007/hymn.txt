Genesis 1:7, this is a hymn. The text is 'And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.'

Hymn 305, 'Amazing Grace,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to rain of grace.
Hymn 183, 'Like a Dry Grass in Empty Fields,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to gentle rain of the Spirit.
Hymn 316, 'Lord, My Life,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to preserving love.
Hymn 170, 'Holy Bible, Book Divine,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to thanksgiving for provision.
