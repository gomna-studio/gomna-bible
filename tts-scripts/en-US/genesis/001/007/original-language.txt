Genesis 1:7, key word analysis. The text is 'And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.'

Asah is a verb meaning to make. Detailed craftsmanship.
Raqia is a noun meaning firmament. The dome of order continues.
Tahat is a preposition meaning under. A structure of authority.
Mayim is a noun meaning waters. Waters above and below are divided.
Ma‘al is a preposition meaning above. Theology of the heights.

This verse shows that God forms the firmament and completes the separation of the waters above and below.
