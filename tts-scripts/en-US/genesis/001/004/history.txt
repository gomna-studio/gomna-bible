Genesis 1:4, historical background. The text is 'And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the light from the darkness.'

We can consider Light–dark dualism. Distinguished from Zoroastrian and similar dualisms. Good and evil are not equal powers.
We can consider Levitical holiness law. Clean and unclean distinctions. Emphasize a set-apart life.
We can consider Structure of the creation account. The verb badal (to separate) is repeated. Teach ordered living.
