Genesis 1:4, this is a hymn. The text is 'And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the light from the darkness.'

Hymn 288, 'Blessed Assurance,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to thanksgiving for His goodness.
Hymn 410, 'Onward, Christian Soldiers,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to a set-apart life.
Hymn 171, 'God’s Only Begotten Son,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to the Tov declaration.
Hymn 49, 'Heralds of Light,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to mission of light.
