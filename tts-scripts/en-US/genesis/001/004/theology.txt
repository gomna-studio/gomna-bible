Genesis 1:4, theological meaning. The text is 'And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the light from the darkness.'

The goodness of God is revealed here. The declaration of Tov is repeated. This can be seen along with Ps 34:8.
Holiness is revealed here. An act of separation. This can be seen along with 1 Pet 1:16.
The foundation of ethics is revealed here. The moral standard is the Creator. This can be seen along with Mic 6:8.
