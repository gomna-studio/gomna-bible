Genesis 1:4, typology. The text is 'And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the light from the darkness.'

From the perspective of OT type, light in the land of Goshen at the Exodus. In connection with Christ, The redeemed community.
From the perspective of NT fulfillment, the light of the fruit of the Spirit. In connection with Christ, Eph 5:9.
From the perspective of Christology, christ separates light from darkness. In connection with Christ, John 3:19-21.
