Genesis 1:4, key word analysis. The verse says, "God saw that the light was good, and God separated the light from the darkness."

"Tov" is an adjective meaning "good." In this verse, it shows that God is the one who determines what is good.
"Badal" is a verb meaning "to divide" or "to separate." Here, it describes God's separation of light from darkness.
"Or" is a noun meaning "light." In Scripture, light can symbolize revelation and life.
"Choshek" is a noun meaning "darkness." Depending on the context, darkness can symbolize sin or disorder.
"Elohim" means "God." Here, it refers to the Creator who judges what is good and establishes order.

This verse shows that God Himself determines what is good and separates light from darkness according to His purpose.
