Genesis 1:2, counseling application. The text is 'The earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was moving over the surface of the waters.'

This passage can be applied to believers who express depression due to a sense of emptiness. The biblical principle is that emptiness, or void, is filled by God's presence. Practical application can include encouraging meditation on the Word and participation in community worship.
This passage can be applied to young people who have lost direction in life. The biblical principle is that the movement of the Spirit, or Ruach, brings order. Practical application can include guiding them to seek the guidance of the Holy Spirit in prayer meetings.
This passage can be applied to people who have failed in business and are living in fear. The biblical principle is that darkness, or hoshech, is also within God's plan. Practical application can include coaching them to reinterpret their failure experiences as testimonies.
