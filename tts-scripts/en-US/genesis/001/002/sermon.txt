Genesis 1:2, sermon material. The text is 'The earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was moving over the surface of the waters.'

The sermon title is 'The Spirit Descending Upon Chaos.' An example of application is the story of an artist turning a blank canvas into a masterpiece.
The introduction confronts us with the chaos and void in our lives. An example of application is the scene before demolition at a moving site.
The first theme is chaos and void. It deals with human existence. An example of application is satellite images wandering the night sky.
The second theme is God covering the darkness. It speaks of the Spirit moving amid darkness and chaos, the presence over chaos. An example of application is a beam of light turning on after a blackout.
The third theme is the movement of the Spirit. It speaks of the beginning of new creation. An example of application is a mother bird incubating an egg just before it hatches.
The conclusion conveys that when we entrust our lives to the Spirit, chaos transforms into mission. An example of application is the case of recycled art.
