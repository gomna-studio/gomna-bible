Genesis 1:2, theological meaning. The text is 'The earth was formless and void, and darkness was over the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was moving over the surface of the waters.'

The meaning of creation faith is revealed. God is the one who brings order out of nothing. This can be seen along with Hebrews 11:3.
The work of the Holy Spirit is also revealed. The Holy Spirit actively participates in the work of creation. This can be seen along with Job 33:4.
The sovereignty of God is also revealed. Chaos and emptiness are under God's rule. This can be seen along with Isaiah 45:18.
