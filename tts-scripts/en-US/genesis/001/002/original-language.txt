Genesis 1:2, key word analysis. The text is 'The earth was without form and void, and darkness was over the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters.'

Tohu is a noun meaning chaos, formlessness. It emphasizes that without God, life is meaningless and formless.
Bohu is a noun meaning emptiness, void. It conveys that the emptiness of the soul is filled only by the presence of God.
Hoshekh is a noun meaning darkness. It makes us think of the spiritual darkness due to sin and the light of God that shines in that darkness.
Tehom is a noun meaning deep, abyss. It emphasizes God working sovereignly even in deep confusion.
Ruach is a noun meaning spirit, wind. It shows the Holy Spirit moving in creation and new creation.

This verse shows that the Spirit of God is at work even in chaos and emptiness.
