Genesis 1:2, typology. The text is 'The earth was without form and void, and darkness was over the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters.'

From the perspective of Old Testament typology, the Spirit hovering over the chaos is connected to the dove after Noah's flood. This also relates to the scene of the Spirit descending at Christ's baptism.
From the perspective of New Testament fulfillment, it connects to the new creation in 2 Corinthians 5:17. In Jesus Christ, chaos is recreated into order.
From the perspective of looking to Christ, it connects to the Word who was with God in the beginning, the Logos, who is Christ. This can be seen in John 1:1-3.
