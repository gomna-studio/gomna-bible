Genesis 1:2, this is a hymn. The text is 'The earth was formless and empty, darkness was over the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was hovering over the waters.'

Hymn 79, 'How Great Thou Art,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn praises the greatness of creation.
Hymn 40, 'How Great Thou Art,' can also be meditated upon together. This hymn brings to mind gratitude for the order of creation.
Hymn 191, 'Because He Lives,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn confesses God's sovereignty even in chaos.
Hymn 452, 'What a Friend We Have in Jesus,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn applies the comfort and movement of the Holy Spirit.
