Genesis 1:2, historical background. The text is 'The earth was formless and empty, darkness was over the surface of the deep, and the Spirit of God was hovering over the waters.'

We can consider ancient Near Eastern creation myths. In Mesopotamian and Ugaritic literature, there are stories of gods conquering the chaotic sea. However, the Bible contrasts this by showing God creating through His word, not through conflict.
We can also consider the editing period of the Pentateuch. The creation faith passed down to the wilderness community after the Exodus becomes a message that encourages pilgrims in chaos to trust the Creator.
The Hebrew cosmology also provides background. The structure of the heavens, earth, and sea, along with the depiction of the Spirit moving over the waters, helps us understand the worldview of the time and opens a dialogue with modern science.
