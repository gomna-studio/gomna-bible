Jeremiah 4:23 is a related Old Testament verse. It connects with the theme of formlessness and emptiness being used again.
Job 26:13 is a related Old Testament verse. It connects with the Spirit's adornment.
Isaiah 45:18 is a related Old Testament verse. It connects with the statement that God did not create the earth in vain.
John 1:1-3 is a related New Testament verse. It connects with the relationship between the Word, or Logos, and creation.
2 Corinthians 4:6 is a related New Testament verse. It connects with the statement of God shining light out of darkness.
Colossians 1:16-17 is a related New Testament verse. It connects with the statement that all things were created through Christ.
Revelation 21:5 is a related New Testament verse. It connects with the statement of making all things new.
