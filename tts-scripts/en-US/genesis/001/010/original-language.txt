Genesis 1:10, key word analysis. The text is 'And God called the dry land Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.'

Erets is a noun meaning earth, land. The stage of life.
Yam is a noun meaning sea. Not an object of ultimate fear.
Qara is a verb meaning to call, name. Authority to name.
Tov is an adjective meaning good. Goodness confirmed.
Miqweh is a noun meaning gathering. A concept of community.

This verse shows that God names earth and seas, and again declares that what He has ordered is good.
