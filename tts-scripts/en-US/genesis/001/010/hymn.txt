Genesis 1:10, this is a hymn. The text is 'And God called the dry land Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.'

Hymn 579, 'Jesus Calls Us,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to calling by the sea.
Hymn 43, 'Thank You, Lord,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to thanksgiving for goodness.
Hymn 304, 'The Love of God,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to sea as a figure.
Hymn 455, 'Stand Up for Jesus,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to steward’s mission.
