Genesis 1:10, historical background. The text is 'And God called the dry land Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.'

We can consider Land–sea concepts. Agrarian versus maritime cultures. Diversity of callings.
We can consider Baal–Yam mythology. Stories of Yam (sea-god) defeated. Contrast with Yahweh’s rule.
We can consider Land inheritance. Promise of the land of Canaan. Trust the fulfillment of promise.
