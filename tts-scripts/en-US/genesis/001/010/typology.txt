Genesis 1:10, typology. The text is 'And God called the dry land Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.'

From the perspective of OT type, inheritance of the promised land. In connection with Christ, Heb 4:8-9.
From the perspective of NT fulfillment, new heaven and new earth. In connection with Christ, Rev 21:1.
From the perspective of Christology, the Lord who stills the sea. In connection with Christ, Mark 4:39.
