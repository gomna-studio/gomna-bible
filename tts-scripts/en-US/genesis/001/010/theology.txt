Genesis 1:10, theological meaning. The text is 'And God called the dry land Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.'

Goodness of creation is revealed here. The Tov declaration is repeated. This can be seen along with Ps 33:5.
Rule by naming is revealed here. Names given to erets and yam. This can be seen along with Ps 29:10.
Stewardship is revealed here. Managers entrusted with the land. This can be seen along with Gen 2:15.
