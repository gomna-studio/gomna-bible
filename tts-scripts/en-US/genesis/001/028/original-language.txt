Genesis 1:28, original language analysis. The text is 'God blessed them and said to them, Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky and over every living creature that moves on the ground.'

The key verb in the text means 'to bless.' It shows that the mission is based on blessing.
The expression 'be fruitful' indicates a command for growth.
The expression 'increase in number' shows quantitative expansion.
The expression 'subdue' indicates the responsibility to establish order.
The expression 'rule' shows delegated governance.
