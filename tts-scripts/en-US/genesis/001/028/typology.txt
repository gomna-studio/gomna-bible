Genesis 1:28, typology. The text is 'God blessed them and said to them, Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky and over every living creature that moves on the ground.'

From the perspective of gospel multiplication, filling the earth is connected to church expansion. This is linked with Acts 9:31.
From the perspective of new creation, subduing and ruling completion is connected. This is linked with Revelation 22:5.
From the perspective of Christ's authority, ruling the sea, sky, and earth is connected. This is linked with Matthew 28:18.
