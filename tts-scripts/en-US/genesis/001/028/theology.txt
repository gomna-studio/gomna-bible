Genesis 1:28, theological meaning. The text is 'God blessed them and said to them, Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky and over every living creature that moves on the ground.'

The meaning of holistic mission is revealed. Being fruitful, subduing, and ruling show spiritual and cultural missions. In this regard, Matthew 28:19 can be viewed together.
The meaning of royal stewardship is revealed. It shows the dual role of king and priest. In this regard, 1 Peter 2:9 can be viewed together.
The meaning of blessing and mission integration is revealed. There is no mission without blessing, and no blessing without mission. In this regard, Genesis 12:2 can be viewed together.
