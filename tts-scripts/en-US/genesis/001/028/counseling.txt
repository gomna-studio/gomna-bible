Genesis 1:28, counseling application. The text is 'God blessed them and said to them, Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky and over every living creature that moves on the ground.'

This message can be applied to a purposeless life. The biblical principle is to hold onto the cultural mandate. A practical application is to suggest a mission statement.
This message can be applied to regret over environmental destruction. The biblical principle is to hold onto care instead of conquest. A practical application is to suggest an eco-friendly life.
This message can be applied to concerns about family planning. The biblical principle is to hold onto fruitfulness and increase. A practical application is to suggest combining prayer and counseling.
