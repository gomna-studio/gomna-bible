Genesis 1:28, sermon material. The text is 'God blessed them and said to them, Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky and over every living creature that moves on the ground.'

The sermon title is Blessing, Fruitfulness, Subduing—Cultural Mandate. An example of application is applying the cultural mandate of blessing, fruitfulness, and subduing in daily life.
In the introduction, the startup mission is discussed. An example of application is a value poster.
Blessed beings. It shows the starting point of grace (God's blessing as the foundation and power of all missions). An example of application is a scholarship story.
Fruitfulness and increase. It shows expansion. An example of application is a franchise.
Subduing and ruling. It shows the restoration of order. An example of application is environmental cleanup.
In conclusion, the message of being a channel of blessing is conveyed. An example of application is social impact.
