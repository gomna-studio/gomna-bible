Genesis 1:12, counseling application. The text is 'The earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed according to their own kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in them, according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.'

This passage can be applied to feelings of worthlessness. The biblical principle is to hold onto 'it was good.' A practical application could be suggesting a gratitude journal of 100 things.
This passage can be applied to loss of vision. The biblical principle is to hold onto the potential within the seed. A practical application could be suggesting creating a vision board.
This passage can be applied to fear of fruit evaluation. The biblical principle is to hold onto God's standard. A practical application could be suggesting a checklist from Galatians 5.
