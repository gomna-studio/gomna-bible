Genesis 1:12, theological meaning. The text is 'The earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed according to their own kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in them, according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.'

The meaning of the completion of good creation is revealed. The repeated 'it was good' shows a pre-conclusion. Relatedly, we can also look at 1 Timothy 4:4.
The meaning of the order of self-replication is revealed. It shows the endowment of reproductive ability in the seed itself. Relatedly, we can also look at Genesis 9:7.
The meaning of a fruitful life is revealed. It shows the purpose of the calling of the saints. Relatedly, we can also look at John 15:8.
