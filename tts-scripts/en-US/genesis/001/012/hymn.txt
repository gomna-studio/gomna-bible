Genesis 1:12, hymn. The text is 'The earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed according to their own kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in them, according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.'

New Hymn 556, 'My Prayer,' can be meditated on together. This hymn is connected to the text in that it reminds us of thanksgiving for the harvest of fruit.
New Hymn 589, 'Lift Up the Name of Jesus,' can be meditated on together. This hymn is connected to the text in that it reminds us of praise for the fruit.
New Hymn 305, 'Amazing Grace,' can be meditated on together. This hymn is connected to the text in that it reminds us of thanksgiving for goodness.
New Hymn 582, 'In the Dark Night,' can be meditated on together. This hymn is connected to the text in that it reminds us of the gospel that bears fruit.
