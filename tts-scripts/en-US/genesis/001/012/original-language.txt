Genesis 1:12, original language analysis. The text is 'The earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed according to their own kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in them, according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.'

Batoce is a perfect verb meaning 'to bring forth.' It shows immediate obedience to the creation command.
Leminehu is a noun meaning 'according to their kinds.' It shows order within diversity.
Zera-lo is a noun meaning 'its seed.' It shows the inherent resource for reproduction.
Tov is an adjective meaning 'good.' It shows that the standard of goodness is God's perspective.
Pri is a noun meaning 'fruit.' It shows that fruit is evidence of obedience.
