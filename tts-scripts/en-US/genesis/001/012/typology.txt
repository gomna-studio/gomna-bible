Genesis 1:12, typology. The text is 'The earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed according to their own kinds, and trees bearing fruit with seed in them, according to their kinds. And God saw that it was good.'

From the perspective of the fruit of the Spirit, good fruit is connected with the result of the Spirit. This is also linked with Matthew 7:17-20.
From the perspective of the expansion of the Gospel, it is connected with the potential of fruit within the seed. This is also linked with Acts 1:8.
From the perspective of the parables of the kingdom, it is connected with the seed growing into fruit. This is also linked with Mark 4:26-29.
