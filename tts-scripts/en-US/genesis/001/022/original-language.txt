Genesis 1:22, original language analysis. The text is 'God blessed them, saying, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.'

The key expression in the text is 'to bless.' Blessing shows the source of multiplication.
The expression 'be fruitful' shows that growth is both a command and a grace.
The expression 'multiply' shows qualitative and quantitative expansion.
The expression 'fill' shows God's command to fill space with life.
The expression 'birds' shows that even the creatures of the air have a calling.
