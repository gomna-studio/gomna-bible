Genesis 1:22, typology. The text is 'God blessed them, saying, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.'

From the perspective of gospel expansion, it connects to fullness to the ends of the earth. This is also connected to Acts 1:8.
From the perspective of the Pentecost 3,000, it connects to a rapidly growing community. This is also connected to Acts 2:41.
From the perspective of the New Jerusalem, it connects to the fullness of the river of life. This is also connected to Revelation 22:1.
