Genesis 1:22, sermon material. The text is 'God blessed them, saying, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.'

The sermon title is Fruitfulness, Multiplication, Fullness – The Three Stages of Blessing. An example of application is applying Fruitfulness, Multiplication, Fullness – The Blessing in daily life.
In the introduction, it compares a fishbowl and an aquarium. An example of application is expansion.
Fruitfulness – The birth of life. It shows the blessing of beginnings (even a small start is a precious possibility of life). An example of application is seed germination.
Multiplication – Growth. It shows the grace of growth (quantitative and qualitative expansion is a natural result of blessing). An example of application is cell growth.
Fullness – Influence. It shows the mission of filling (filling the world with God's glory). An example of application is salt and light.
In the conclusion, it delivers the message to fill the world as blessed beings. An example of application is missionary statistics.
