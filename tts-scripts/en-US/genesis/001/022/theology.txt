Genesis 1:22, theological meaning. The text is 'God blessed them, saying, Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let birds multiply on the earth.'

The meaning of the theology of blessing is revealed. Blessing shows the order of creation. Related to this, we can also look at James 1:17.
The meaning of life expansion is revealed. Being fruitful and multiplying shows the purpose of creation. Related to this, we can also look at Genesis 9:7.
The meaning of the theology of fullness is revealed. It shows the missionary mandate. Related to this, we can also look at Ephesians 1:23.
