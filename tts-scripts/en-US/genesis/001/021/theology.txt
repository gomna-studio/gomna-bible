Genesis 1:21, theological significance. The text is 'God created the great sea creatures and every living creature that moves, with which the waters swarm, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

The meaning of sovereign creation is revealed. The reappearance of the verb bará shows unique creation. Related to this, Isaiah 40:26 can be considered.
The meaning of the blessing of diversity is revealed. The repetition of 'according to their kinds' relates to the goodness of differences. Related to this, 1 Corinthians 12:4-6 can be considered.
The meaning of ecological stewardship is revealed. It relates to the responsibility of managing creation. Related to this, Genesis 2:15 can be considered.
