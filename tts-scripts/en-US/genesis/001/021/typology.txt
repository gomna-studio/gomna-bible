Genesis 1:21, typology. The text is 'God created the great sea creatures and every living creature that moves, with which the waters swarm, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

From Jonah's perspective with the great fish, it connects to the path of salvation. This is linked to Matthew 12:40.
From Peter's perspective with the great fish, it connects to the abundance of 153 fish. This is linked to John 21:11.
From the perspective of the sea beast in Revelation, it connects to the subject of judgment. This is linked to Revelation 13:1.
