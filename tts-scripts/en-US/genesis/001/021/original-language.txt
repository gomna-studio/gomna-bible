Genesis 1:21, original language analysis. The text is 'God created the great sea creatures and every living creature that moves, with which the waters swarm, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

The key expression in the text is the noun meaning 'great sea monsters.' It shows that even objects of fear are creatures.
The key verb in the text is the perfect verb meaning 'created.' Creation ex nihilo shows God's domain.
Another key expression is the noun meaning 'living being.' It shows the breath of life.
The expression 'according to their kinds' is a combination of a preposition and plural form. It shows the order of diversity.
The expression 'it was good' is an adjective, showing the good order by God's standard.
