Genesis 1:21, historical background. The text is 'God created the great sea creatures and every living creature that moves, with which the waters swarm, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

We can consider the Leviathan myth. The Canaanite Baal myth's sea monster is demoted to a creature in the Bible. This prompts a gospel response to fear myths.
We can consider fish and bird classification. Ancient people had a background of recognizing kinds—linked to prohibition and purity laws. This prompts acceptance of multicultural and multiracial diversity.
We can consider the profession of Galilean fishermen. There is a background of the fishing economy in Jesus' time. This prompts reflection on the value of vocational calling.
