Genesis 1:21, counseling application. The text is 'God created the great sea creatures and every living creature that moves, with which the waters swarm, according to their kinds, and every winged bird according to its kind. And God saw that it was good.'

This passage can be applied to fear and anxiety. The biblical principle is to hold onto the fact that even great beasts are under sovereignty. A practical application could be a fear list-prayer response.
This passage can be applied to a sense of discrimination. The biblical principle is to hold onto the goodness of kinds. A practical application could be participating in multicultural fellowship meetings.
This passage can be applied to environmental indifference. The biblical principle is to hold onto the responsibility of stewardship. A practical application could be a zero waste challenge.
