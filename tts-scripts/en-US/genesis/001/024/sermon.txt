Genesis 1:24, sermon material. The text is 'And God said, Let the earth bring forth living creatures after their kind, cattle, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth after their kind: and it was so.'

The sermon title is Life of the Earth—Cooperation and Reverence. An example of application is applying the life of the earth—cooperation and reverence in daily life.
In the introduction, it talks about the cooperation of farmers and shepherds. An example of application is plowing with oxen.
Cattle. It shows cooperation. An example of application is milk production.
Creeping things. It shows humility. An example of application is ant colonies.
Wild. It shows reverence. An example of application is a safari.
In conclusion, it conveys a message of harmony with creation. An example of application is ecological farming.
