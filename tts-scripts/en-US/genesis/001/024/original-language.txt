Genesis 1:24, original language analysis. The text is 'And God said, Let the earth bring forth living creatures after their kind, cattle, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth after their kind: and it was so.'

The key expression in the text is 'bring forth.' It shows that the earth is used as a partner in creation.
The expression 'living creature' shows that creatures share the breath of life.
The expression 'cattle' shows the order of cooperation with humans.
The expression 'creeping things' shows that even small beings should not be ignored.
The expression 'beasts of the earth' shows the beauty of the wild.
