Genesis 1:24, Matthew Henry. The text is 'And God said, Let the earth bring forth living creatures after their kind, cattle, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth after their kind: and it was so.'

Matthew Henry's English text.
The earth is a nurse to all.
The earth is a nurse to all life. The key here is that it shows interdependence.
Second English text.
Creatures suited to various services.
They are suited to different purposes. The key here is that it shows the diversity of gifts.
Final English text.
Even creeping things have place.
Even creeping things have a place. The key here is that it shows there is no insignificance.
