Genesis 1:24, counseling application. The text is 'And God said, Let the earth bring forth living creatures after their kind, cattle, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth after their kind: and it was so.'

This passage can be applied to those feeling guilt over environmental destruction. The biblical principle is to hold onto stewardship responsibility. A practical application could be reducing carbon emissions.
This passage can be applied to those with low self-esteem. The biblical principle is to hold onto the value of even creeping things. A practical application could be discovering one's mission.
This passage can be applied to those who undervalue their job. The biblical principle is to hold onto the value of cattle labor. A practical application could be redefining one's calling.
