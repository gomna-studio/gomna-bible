Genesis 1:24, historical background. The text is 'And God said, Let the earth bring forth living creatures after their kind, cattle, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth after their kind: and it was so.'

We can consider nomadic culture. Cattle were seen as property and sacrifice. This point leads us to think of stewardship rather than ownership.
We can consider purity and impurity. There is a background of animal classification in Leviticus 11. This point leads us to think of holy distinction.
We can consider the necessity of agriculture. There is a background of cattle labor and fertilizer. This point leads us to think of the value of labor.
