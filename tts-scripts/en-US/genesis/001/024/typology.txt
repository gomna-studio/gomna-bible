Genesis 1:24, typology. The text is 'And God said, Let the earth bring forth living creatures after their kind, cattle, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth after their kind: and it was so.'

From the perspective of the sacrificial lamb, it connects to sheep among cattle. This relates to John 1:29.
From the perspective of the serpent symbol, it connects to snakes among creeping things. This relates to Numbers 21:9.
From the perspective of the lion symbol, it connects to wild beasts. This relates to Revelation 5:5.
