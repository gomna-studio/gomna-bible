Genesis 1:3, original-language analysis. The text is, "God said, 'Let there be light,' and there was light."

Amar is a verb meaning to speak. This word shows that God's word is not just a sound but a power that actually accomplishes things.
Yehi is an imperative verb meaning 'let there be.' This imperative expression shows that the created world responds immediately to God's word.
Or is a noun meaning 'light.' Here, light can be read as an important symbol that evokes revelation and salvation.
Wayehi is a perfect-form verb meaning 'to come to be.' This expression shows that what God spoke was actually fulfilled.
Elohim is a noun meaning God, and though it is plural, it is used with a singular meaning. This name emphasizes the sovereignty of God the Creator.

This verse shows the power of God's word to create light.
