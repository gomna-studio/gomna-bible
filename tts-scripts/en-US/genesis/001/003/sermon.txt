Genesis 1:3, sermon material. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

The sermon title is Let There Be Light! A word that illuminates chaos. An example of application is switching on a flash in a darkroom.
The introduction talks about modern people lost in darkness. An example of application is the experience of a blackout.
The first point is God's voice opening creation. The power of the Word is revealed in that God's Word begins creation. An example of application is the conductor's first beat.
The second point is the gift of light. As light is given, order begins in the created world. An example of application is the first rays of dawn.
The third point is the gospel shining in my life today. Our lives today also need the light of the gospel. Just as one finds light at the end of a dark tunnel, God's Word gives direction and hope to those in despair.
The conclusion delivers the message to proclaim Christ, the light. An example of application is a candlelight relay.
