Genesis chapter 1 verse 3, Matthew Henry. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

This is Matthew Henry's original English text.
God's word created the first daybreak.
This sentence means that God's word created the first dawn. The key point here is that God's word actually has the power of creation.
This is the second original English text.
No creature can resist His fiat.
This sentence means that no creature can resist His command. This expression shows that all creatures are under God's command.
This is the last original English text.
Light is the first blessing He bestows.
This sentence means that light is the first blessing God gives. The fact that light was given first makes us think about how God's grace precedes human response.
