Genesis 1:3, cross-reference. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

Psalm 119:105 is an Old Testament cross-reference. This verse connects to Genesis 1:3 in that God's word is a lamp to our path.
Isaiah 60:1 is an Old Testament cross-reference. This verse shows the mission of God's light through the call to arise and shine.
Job 38:19 is an Old Testament cross-reference. This verse asks about the dwelling of light, prompting us to think of God as the source of light.
Malachi 4:2 is an Old Testament cross-reference. This verse uses the image of the sun of righteousness rising to show God's salvation and restoration.
John 8:12 is a New Testament cross-reference. Jesus saying He is the light of the world connects to the light in Genesis.
2 Corinthians 4:6 is a New Testament cross-reference. This verse explains that God, who shone light in darkness, also shines the glory of Christ in our hearts.
Ephesians 5:8-9 is a New Testament cross-reference. This verse leads to the application that believers should live as children of light.
Revelation 22:5 is a New Testament cross-reference. This verse shows the eternal light God provides, prompting thoughts of creation's light leading to the new creation's completion.
