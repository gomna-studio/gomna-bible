Genesis 1:3, historical background. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

We can consider the ancient Near Eastern concept of light. In the ancient Near East, there was a belief that the sun or light was like a god. However, Genesis 1:3 shows that light is also a creation made by God. Therefore, we should not worship the creation, but look to God who made the light.
We can think about Israel's wilderness experience. God led Israel with a pillar of fire, showing them the way even in the darkness. This background connects to the fact that God's word is like a light guiding the people.
We can consider the editorial purpose of Genesis. For the Israelite community after the Exodus, this message instilled an important identity. God is the one who opens a new beginning even in darkness and is the Creator who illuminates the path with His word.
