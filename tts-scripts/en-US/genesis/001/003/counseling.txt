Genesis 1:3, counseling application. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

This passage can be applied to someone experiencing spiritual darkness. The biblical principle is to accept the word of light. A practical application could be to suggest the practice of daily meditation on the Word.
This passage can be applied to a wandering young person. The biblical principle is to set the direction of life by following the light. A practical application could be to consider connecting them with a faith mentor.
This passage can be applied to someone struggling with feelings of depression. The biblical principle is to look for the hope of light even in darkness. A practical application could be to suggest the practice of keeping a gratitude journal to record small lights.
