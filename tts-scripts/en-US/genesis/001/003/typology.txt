Genesis 1:3, typology. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

From the perspective of Old Testament typology, this statement evokes the light given during the Exodus in the midst of darkness. It also connects to the scene of salvation through the blood of the lamb in the darkness.
From the perspective of New Testament fulfillment, this statement connects to Jesus Christ declaring Himself as the light. In this context, John 8:12 can be considered.
From the perspective of Christology, the theme of the Word shining from the beginning is revealed. In this context, 2 Corinthians 4:6 can be considered.
