Genesis 1:3, the theological meaning. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

The meaning of revelation is revealed. Light is connected to the way God reveals Himself. In this regard, John 1:4-5 can be viewed together.
The meaning of the doctrine of creation is revealed. This statement shows that God creates something from nothing by His word. In this regard, Hebrews 11:3 can be viewed together.
The meaning of the Word Christology is revealed. The fact that existence comes into being by God's word is connected to Christ who creates by the Word. In this regard, Colossians 1:16 can be viewed together.
