Genesis 1:3, this is a hymn. The text is 'And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.'

We can meditate on New Hymnal 79, 'O Lord my God, when I in awesome wonder.' This hymn fits well with the text as it praises creation and light.
We can meditate on New Hymnal 49, 'Ye Messengers of Light.' This hymn connects with the text as it proclaims the light of the gospel.
We can meditate on New Hymnal 288, 'Blessed Assurance, Jesus is Mine.' This hymn makes us think of the grace of light as it sings of the assurance of salvation.
We can meditate on New Hymnal 191, 'Because He Lives.' This hymn connects with the text as it acknowledges the sovereignty of the living Lord.
