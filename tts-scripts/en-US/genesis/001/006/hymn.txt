Genesis 1:6, this is a hymn. The text is 'And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.'

Hymn 40, 'How Great Thou Art,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to the order of nature.
Hymn 79, 'How Great Thou Art,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to praise of the firmament.
Hymn 363, 'It Is Well with My Soul,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to peace in the storm.
Hymn 438, 'O God, Our Help in Ages Past,' can be meditated upon together. This hymn relates to safety through mediation.
