Genesis 1:6, historical background. The text is 'And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.'

We can consider Ancient dome cosmology. The firmament as a sky-dome. Understand the culture, then emphasize the truth.
We can consider Contrast with Babylonian myth. The firmament formed from Tiamat’s corpse. Order by the Word, not by violence.
We can consider Waters as chaos. The deep (tehom). Preach overcoming fear.
