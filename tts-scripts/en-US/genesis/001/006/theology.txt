Genesis 1:6, theological meaning. The text is 'And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.'

Theology of order is revealed here. Creation is an act of separation and distinction. This can be seen along with 1 Cor 14:33.
Doctrine of providence is revealed here. Rule over the chaos. This can be seen along with Ps 29:10.
Doctrine of heaven is revealed here. The meaning of creating the heavens. This can be seen along with Isa 66:1.
