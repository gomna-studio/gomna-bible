Genesis 1:6, key word analysis. The text is 'And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.'

Raqia is a noun meaning firmament, expanse. A space of order.
Betokh is a preposition meaning in the midst. Mediation and center.
Mayim is a noun meaning waters. An element of chaos.
Hibadel is a piel imperative meaning be divided. A command to separate.
Yehi is a jussive meaning let there be. A creative declaration.

This verse shows that God speaks a separating expanse into being, setting boundaries within the waters.
