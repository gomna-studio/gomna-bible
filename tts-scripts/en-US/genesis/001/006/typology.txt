Genesis 1:6, typology. The text is 'And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.'

From the perspective of OT type, the parting of the Red Sea. In connection with Christ, A type of baptism.
From the perspective of NT fulfillment, the heavens opened at Jesus’ baptism. In connection with Christ, Matt 3:16.
From the perspective of Christology, the Mediator connecting heaven and earth. In connection with Christ, John 1:51.
