Genesis 1:19, counseling application. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.'

This verse can be applied to fatigue from perfectionism. The biblical principle is to hold onto progressive completion. A practical application is to break goals into daily, weekly, and monthly stages.
This verse can be applied to disrupted life rhythms. The biblical principle is to hold onto the evening-morning pattern. A practical application is to fix bedtime and wake-up time.
This verse can be applied to impatience. The biblical principle is to hold onto God's timeline. A practical application is to focus on the present with a gratitude journal.
