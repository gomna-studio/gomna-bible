Genesis 1:19, sermon material. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.'

The sermon title is The Message of the Fourth Day – Rhythm and Patience. An example of application is applying the message of the fourth day – rhythm and patience in daily life.
In the introduction, the four stages of film production (pre→post) are discussed. An example of application is a sketch of the filming site.
The wisdom of evening – stopping. It shows the grace of letting go (the wisdom of ending and resting for the day). An example of application is the 'night mode' on a phone.
The hope of morning – beginning. It shows the opportunity for a fresh start (the new dawn of grace given daily). An example of application is a sunrise hike.
The blessing of stages – the fourth day. It shows the meaning of process (God's work that is not hurried but leaves nothing unfinished). An example of application is a 60% progress report in construction.
In conclusion, the message is to walk with God's tempo. An example of application is metronome training.
