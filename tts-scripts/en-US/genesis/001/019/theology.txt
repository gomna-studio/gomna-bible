Genesis 1:19, theological significance. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.'

The meaning of sovereignty over time is revealed. The ruler of the structure of the day. Related to this, see Daniel 2:21.
The meaning of progressive sanctification is revealed. Repeated pattern until the fourth day—'already, not yet.' Related to this, see 2 Corinthians 3:18.
The meaning of the goodness of creation is revealed. Daily proclamation affirms creation. Related to this, see Genesis 1:31.
