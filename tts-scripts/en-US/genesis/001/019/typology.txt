Genesis 1:19, typology. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the fourth day.'

From the perspective of Jesus as the light, the completion of the movement of light bodies on the fourth day is the preparation for the Messiah's light. This connects with Malachi 4:2.
From the perspective of the resurrection morning, the evening-morning repetition is a foreshadowing of the resurrection at dawn on the third day. This connects with Mark 16:2.
From the perspective of new creation, it is a foreshadowing of a new heaven without night. This connects with Revelation 22:5.
