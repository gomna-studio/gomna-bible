Genesis 1:15, counseling application. The text is 'And let them be lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth'; and it was so.

This verse can be applied to spiritual depression. The biblical principle is to hold onto receiving light to shine. A practical application is a 20-minute scripture-sunlight walk.
This verse can be applied to relational conflict. The biblical principle is to hold onto light as revelation. A practical application is an honest conversation light room time.
This verse can be applied to low self-esteem. The biblical principle is to hold onto the fulfillment of God's word. A practical application is the Lord's promise stickers.
