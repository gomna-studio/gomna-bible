Genesis 1:15, theological meaning. The text is 'And let them be lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth'; and it was so.

The meaning of the transmission of revelation is revealed. Light shows the medium of truth and grace. Relatedly, John 1:9 can be viewed together.
The meaning of mission is revealed. The light-receiving saints – show the mission to illuminate. Relatedly, Matthew 5:14-16 can be viewed together.
The meaning of faithfulness is revealed. 'And it was so' shows the fulfillment of the word. Relatedly, Isaiah 55:11 can be viewed together.
