Genesis 1:15, original language analysis. The text is 'And let them be lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth'; and it was so.

V'hayu is a perfect verb meaning 'let them be.' It shows the assignment of purpose.
L'm'orot means 'for lights,' a preposition + plural. It shows various light sources.
L'ha'ir means 'to give light,' in Hiphil. It shows the active mission of illumination.
Al-ha'aretz means 'on the earth,' a prepositional phrase. It shows the world as the target of light.
Vayehi-ken means 'and it was so.' It shows the immediate connection from word to reality.
