Genesis 1:15, historical background. The text is 'And let them be lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth'; and it was so.

We can consider agricultural lighting. Sunlight influences production and health. This point makes us think of spiritual vitamin D.
We can consider the symbolism of lamps. The tabernacle's golden lampstand – its role in illuminating the earth. This point makes us think of the church's mission to illuminate the world.
We can consider the image of royal light. Ancient kings were called the sun, and the Bible presents serving light. This point makes us think of servant leadership.
