Genesis 1:15, hymn. The text is 'And let them be lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth'; and it was so.

New Hymnal 191, 'As Surely as the Lord Lives,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in reminding us of the mission of light.
New Hymnal 255, 'The Lord, the Light,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in reminding us of Jesus as the true light.
New Hymnal 200, 'The Lord God Made Them All,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in reminding us of the praise of creation's light.
New Hymnal 94, 'Joy to the World,' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects with the text in reminding us of gratitude for the light's shining.
