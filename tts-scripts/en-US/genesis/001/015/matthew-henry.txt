Genesis 1:15, Matthew Henry. The text is 'And let them be lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth'; and it was so.

Matthew Henry's English original.
Light is sweet; so are the consolations of the gospel.
As light is sweet, so are the consolations of the gospel. The key here is showing the connection between light and the gospel.
Second English original.
God made lights, not to be worshipped, but serviceable.
Lights are not objects of worship but tools of service. The key here is showing instrumental value.
Final English original.
The lights in heaven preach to us night and day.
The lights in heaven preach to us night and day. The key here is showing natural revelation.
