Genesis 1:15, typology. The text is 'And let them be lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth'; and it was so.

From the perspective of Christ as the light of the world, it connects to His coming as the true light. This is linked with John 8:12.
From the perspective of saints as lamps, it connects to the preparation for the wedding feast with lamps. This is linked with Matthew 25:1-13.
From the perspective of the New Jerusalem, it connects to the Lamb as the light. This is linked with Revelation 22:5.
