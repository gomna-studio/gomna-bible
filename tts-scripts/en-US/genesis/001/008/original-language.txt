Genesis 1:8, key word analysis. The text is 'And God called the firmament Heaven. And the evening and the morning were the second day.'

Qara is a verb meaning to call, name. Sovereignty in naming.
Shamayim is a plural noun meaning heaven. Height and transcendence.
Erev is a noun meaning evening. The beginning of rest.
Boqer is a noun meaning morning. A new opportunity.
Yom sheni is a compound meaning the second day. Process matters.

This verse shows that God names the expanse Heaven, and that the second day closes under His faithful order.
