Genesis 1:8, theological meaning. The text is 'And God called the firmament Heaven. And the evening and the morning were the second day.'

Theology of kingship is revealed here. Rule expressed by naming. This can be seen along with Isa 40:26.
Theology of process is revealed here. No “good” on day two—patience with the unfinished. This can be seen along with Phil 1:6.
Heavenly citizenship is revealed here. Hope of the shamayim. This can be seen along with Phil 3:20.
