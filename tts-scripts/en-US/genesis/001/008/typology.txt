Genesis 1:8, typology. The text is 'And God called the firmament Heaven. And the evening and the morning were the second day.'

From the perspective of OT type, jacob’s ladder connecting to heaven. In connection with Christ, An image of mediation.
From the perspective of NT fulfillment, ascension and seating in heaven. In connection with Christ, Acts 1:9.
From the perspective of Christology, he holds the true keys of heaven. In connection with Christ, Matt 16:19.
