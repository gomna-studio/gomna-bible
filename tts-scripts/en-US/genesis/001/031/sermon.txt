Genesis 1:31, sermon material. The text is 'God saw all that He had made, and it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.'

The sermon title is Very Good – The Aesthetics of Completion. An example of application is applying Very Good – The Aesthetics of Completion in daily life.
The introduction talks about the architect's final inspection. An example of application is the completion of a skyline.
God's satisfaction. It shows the Creator's joy (God who is satisfied seeing all creation). An example of application is winning first place in a work evaluation.
Our rest. It shows rest after completion (the blessed invitation to rest after labor). An example of application is a vacation after graduation.
Hope for the incomplete. It shows the expectation of new creation (hope to be fully restored in Christ). An example of application is before and after remodeling.
The conclusion delivers the message of 'It is finished' life. An example of application is checking off a bucket list.
