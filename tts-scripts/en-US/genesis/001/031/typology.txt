Genesis 1:31, typology. The text is 'God saw all that He had made, and it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.'

From the perspective of the cross 'It is finished,' creation completion connects to redemption completion. This also connects to John 19:30.
From the perspective of new creation 'became new,' very good connects to new creation. This also connects to 2 Corinthians 5:17.
From the perspective of eternal rest, it connects to the shadow of the 7th day rest. This also connects to Hebrews 4:9-10.
