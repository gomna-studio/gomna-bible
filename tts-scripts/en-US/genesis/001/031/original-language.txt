Genesis 1:31, original language analysis. The text is 'God saw all that He had made, and it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.'

Vayar is a perfect tense verb meaning 'saw.' It shows the evaluating Creator.
Kol Asher Asa means 'all that He had made.' It shows comprehensive gratitude.
Tov Meod means 'very good.' It shows completed goodness.
Erev means 'evening.' It shows an invitation to rest.
Boker means 'morning.' It shows preparation for a new start.
