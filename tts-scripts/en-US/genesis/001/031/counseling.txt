Genesis 1:31, counseling application. The text is 'God saw all that He had made, and it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.'

This passage can be applied to the pressure of perfectionism. The biblical principle is to hold onto God's completion and human rest. A practical application is to suggest the '80% satisfaction with results' rule.
This passage can be applied to the emptiness after achievement. The biblical principle is to hold onto the joy of 'very good.' A practical application is to suggest a gratitude retreat.
This passage can be applied to rest deficiency. The biblical principle is to hold onto the pattern of rest. A practical application is to suggest a weekly Sabbath plan.
