We can consider the declaration of completion. There is a background of the ancient construction dedication 'good' proclamation. This makes us think of a project completion worship.
We can consider the 6+1 pattern. There is a background of the foundation of 6 days of labor and 1 day of rest. This makes us think of restoring weekly rhythm.
We can consider the emphasis on 'very.' There is a background of the Hebrew hyperbole Meod usage. This makes us think of God's standard of satisfaction.
