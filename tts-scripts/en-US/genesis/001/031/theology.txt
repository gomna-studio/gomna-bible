Genesis 1:31, theological meaning. The text is 'God saw all that He had made, and it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day.'

The meaning of creation completion is revealed. 'Very' shows a declaration of perfection. In relation, we can also see Hebrews 4:3.
The meaning of rest preparation is revealed. It shows the 7-day rest after evening-morning. In relation, we can also see Genesis 2:2.
The meaning of moral ethics is revealed. God's evaluation shows the moral standard. In relation, we can also see Micah 6:8.
