Genesis 1:5, key word analysis. The text is 'And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And the evening and the morning were the first day.'

Qara is a verb meaning to call, name. Authority to name.
Yom is a noun meaning day. Time for activity and life.
Laylah is a noun meaning night. Rest and trust.
Erev is a noun meaning evening. The Jewish day begins here.
Boqer is a noun meaning morning. A hopeful new beginning.

This verse shows that God names light and darkness, and that time itself begins under His ordering word.
