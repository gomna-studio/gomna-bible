Genesis 1:5, typology. The text is 'And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And the evening and the morning were the first day.'

From the perspective of OT type, from Passover night to daylight Exodus. In connection with Christ, A pattern of salvation.
From the perspective of NT fulfillment, from the night of the cross to the dawn of resurrection. In connection with Christ, Light beyond death.
From the perspective of Christology, christ the Lord of days. In connection with Christ, Rev 1:8.
