Genesis 1:5, theological meaning. The text is 'And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And the evening and the morning were the first day.'

Doctrine of time is revealed here. Time itself is part of creation. This can be seen along with Eccl 3:1.
Doctrine of Sabbath is revealed here. Evening comes first in the order. This can be seen along with Lev 23:32.
Doctrine of providence is revealed here. Night and day alike are under His rule. This can be seen along with Ps 139:11-12.
