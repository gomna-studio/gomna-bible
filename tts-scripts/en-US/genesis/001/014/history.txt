Genesis 1:14, historical background. The text is 'And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years.'

Consider the refutation of astrology worship. There is a background of demoting the sun, moon, and stars worshiped as gods. This point makes us think of worshiping the Creator instead of idols.
Consider the Hebrew festivals. There is a background that the calendar, including Passover and Tabernacles, is based on celestial cycles. This point makes us think of the creative roots of worship schedules.
Consider the agricultural clock. There is a background that the division of seasons is essential for sowing and harvesting. This point makes us think of timing in ministry.
