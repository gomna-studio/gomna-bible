Genesis 1:14, original language analysis. The text is 'And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years.'

Meorot is a noun meaning 'light-bodies.' It shows the installation of heavenly lamps.
Lehavdil means 'to separate,' in Hiphil. Light shows the gift of discernment.
Ototh is a noun meaning 'signs.' The order of creation shows a message.
Moadim means 'appointed times,' plural of festivals. It shows the origin of the worship calendar.
Shanim is a noun meaning 'years.' It shows long-term time management.
