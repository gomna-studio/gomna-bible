Genesis 1:14, typology. The text is 'And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years.'

From the perspective of the sign of the Messiah, the star is connected to the announcement of the king's birth. This connects to Matthew 2:2.
From the perspective of the darkness of the cross, the sun losing its light is connected to a sign of judgment. This connects to Luke 23:44-45.
From the perspective of the light of the new creation, it is connected to the city that does not need the sun or moon. This connects to Revelation 21:23.
