Genesis 1:14, theological meaning. The text is 'And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years.'

The meaning of the providence of schedules is revealed. It shows God who sets the standard of time. In relation, see Daniel 2:21.
The meaning of revelatory signs is revealed. Signs show the alarm of redemptive history. In relation, see Luke 21:25.
The meaning of worship rhythm is revealed. Festivals show the distinction of holy time. In relation, see Leviticus 23:2.
