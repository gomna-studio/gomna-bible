Genesis 1:14, hymn. The text is 'And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years.'

New Hymn 64, 'Joyful, Joyful, We Adore Thee' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text as it reminds us of the praise of creation and providence.
New Hymn 36, 'All Hail the Power of Jesus' Name' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text as it reminds us of exalting the Lord instead of idols.
New Hymn 191, 'As the Deer' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text as it reminds us of the mission of the light of the gospel.
New Hymn 446, 'Leaning on the Everlasting Arms' can be meditated upon. This hymn connects to the text as it reminds us of relying on the Word over signs.
