Genesis 1:13, original language analysis. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.'

וַֽיְהִי־עֶ֥רֶב is a perfect verb meaning 'there was evening.' The end also shows the door to the next beginning.
וַֽיְהִי־בֹ֖קֶר is a perfect verb meaning 'there was morning.' The new light after darkness shows God's rhythm.
י֥וֹם means 'day,' masculine singular. Time also belongs to the Creator.
שְּׁלִישִׁי is an adjective meaning 'third.' It shows the progressive completion in the process.
הָיָה is a perfect verb meaning 'to be.' It shows the fulfillment of God's word.
