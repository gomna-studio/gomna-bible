Genesis 1:13, typology. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.'

From the perspective of Jesus' resurrection, the completion on the third day connects to new life. This is linked with 1 Corinthians 15:4.
From the perspective of the sign of Jonah, it connects to restoration in three days. This is linked with Matthew 12:40.
From the perspective of the journey of sanctification, it connects to the process-completion pattern. This is linked with 2 Corinthians 3:18.
