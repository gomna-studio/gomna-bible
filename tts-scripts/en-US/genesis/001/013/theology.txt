Genesis 1:13, theological meaning. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.'

The meaning of stewardship of time is revealed. The evening-morning cycle shows the balance of life and rest. In relation, we can look at Psalm 90:12.
The meaning of process theology is revealed. The creation progressing until the third day shows the faith education of 'not yet.' In relation, we can look at Philippians 1:6.
The meaning of God's satisfaction is revealed. The declaration of 'good' reflects the joy of the Creator. In relation, we can look at Revelation 4:11.
