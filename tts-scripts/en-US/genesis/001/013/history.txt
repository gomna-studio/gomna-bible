Genesis 1:13, historical background. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.'

We can consider the Hebrew calendar. There is a background of calculating the start of the day with sunset. This point makes us think of the focus of rest starting with preparation.
We can consider the seven-day cycle. There is a background that the Near Eastern culture also had a seven-day cycle but with different meanings. This point makes us think of the gospel reinterpretation of the Sabbath.
We can consider the third-day 'good' repetition. There is a background of the third day having two 'good' declarations. This point makes us think of the connection to wedding traditions as a day of fruition.
