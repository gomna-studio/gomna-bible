Genesis 1:13, Matthew Henry. The text is 'And there was evening, and there was morning—the third day.'

Matthew Henry's English text.
Every evening brings us nearer to our rest.
Evening leads us closer to rest. The key here is that it shows rhythmic spirituality.
Second English text.
God's work goes on by steps.
God's work is step-by-step. The key here is that it shows steps even in calling.
Final English text.
Providence still provides.
Providence still supplies. The key here is that it shows trust in provision.
