두 번째 영어 원문입니다.
Thorns were the crown that Christ wore to deliver us from this curse.

The herb of the field—instead of the fruit of paradise, now common vegetables.

마지막 영어 원문입니다.
Man must now labour hard for what once came easily in Eden.
